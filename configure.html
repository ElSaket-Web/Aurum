Frequently Asked Questions

What is the required deposit to secure a build allocation?

A 20% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your exclusive chassis number and begin the bespoke tailoring process.

When will delivery commence?

Deliveries for the E-GT begin in Q3 2026 for North American markets, followed by Europe and Asia in Q4 2026. Custom bespoke options may add 4-6 weeks to production time.

Does the vehicle come with a home charging solution?

Yes. Every E-GT includes a complimentary 22kW AURUM Wallbox installed at your residence by our certified network of electricians.

What kind of warranty is included?

The E-GT is backed by a 4-year/50,000-mile comprehensive warranty, and an industry-leading 10-year unlimited-mile warranty on the high-voltage battery architecture.

Can I test drive the E-GT before finalizing?

Private track days and VIP test drives are arranged exclusively for deposit-holders through our Concierge service in select global hubs (Geneva, Los Angeles, Dubai).
