Engineering Excellence

Aerodynamic Purity

Form follows function. The E-GT boasts a drag coefficient of 0.22 Cd. Every intake and line serves to cool the battery thermal management system while maximizing high-speed stability.

Carbon-Composite Chassis

To offset battery weight, the central tub is constructed from motorsport-grade carbon fiber. This ensures exceptional torsional rigidity and razor-sharp steering response.

Unsprung Mass Reduction

Optional 21-inch forged magnesium wheels reduce rotational mass by 12kg. Paired with carbon ceramic brakes, the dynamic feedback is unparalleled.

Tailored Interior

The cabin eschews unnecessary digital distraction for analog elegance. Hand-stitched leather, tactile physical dials, and an unobtrusive driver display center the focus on the road.
